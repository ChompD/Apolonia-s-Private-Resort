About Us

From a family rest house to a private resort in Angeles City.

Family

Our story

Angeles City, Philippines · Since 2022

Apolonia's Private Resort was created in 2022 in Barangay Cuayan as a rest house where the Toribio family could hold family events, and it later became a private resort.

With lush greenery, a spacious pool and modern amenities, it's ideal for intimate gatherings and special occasions with family, friends and guests. Whether you're planning a day swim, an overnight retreat or a memorable celebration, Apolonia's Private Resort delivers the experience you want.

The concept: personal service that combines the warmth of a family home and the luxury of a private resort.
