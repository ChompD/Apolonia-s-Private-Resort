Apolonia

Your own private pool

Two pools, a villa and a function hall, just for your group!

If you are looking for a private, relaxing and comfortable place for your next family event or barkada party, Apolonia's Private Resort in Barangay Cuayan, Angeles City is for you.

Kids get their own 2.5 ft pool while adults enjoy the 4 ft pool. There's a spacious villa with three air-conditioned bedrooms, a function hall with a stage, and a big parking area for your guests.

Find out more

Swim, play and celebrate!

Slides, swings, volleyball, basketball, karaoke and a campfire, all included in your stay.

Stay

Stay the day, or the night

Day swimming from ₱9,000 · Overnight from ₱12,000

Book the resort for a day swim, or stay overnight and wake up by the pool. The villa has a clean, spacious living room, a kitchen with a refrigerator and basic utensils, and a dirty kitchen and barbecue grill for cooking.

The concept: the warmth of a family home with the luxury of a private resort.
